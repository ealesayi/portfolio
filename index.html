Profile

Engineer first, designer by habit.

I am a software engineer with a dual BSc from the UK and Malaysia, working across the full delivery path: interface, API integration, and release. My work today sits with Jaddaf, where I build client web platforms and mobile apps, after three months at Ashal building an Arabic-first Angular system.

Most of what I ship serves Arabic and English audiences at once, so right-to-left layout, bilingual content, and typography that holds up in both scripts are not an afterthought — they are the starting constraint.

I worked as a web developer at KidoCode and delivered freelance Flutter apps and business websites for tailoring shops, pharmacies, and car-care studios. That mix taught me to scope small, ship fast, and keep the interface honest about what it can do.
